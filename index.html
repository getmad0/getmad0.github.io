YouTube Uploader privacy policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

YouTube Uploader is a private tool that runs only on its owner's own computer. It uploads videos and their details to YouTube channels that the owner personally manages. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the tool accesses

With the channel owner's permission, granted through Google sign-in, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload videos to the owner's own channels,
  • set titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, publish schedules and playlists,
  • read the basic channel name and handle, to confirm each upload goes to the right channel.

How data is stored

Sign-in tokens and upload logs are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's own YouTube services. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google and YouTube terms

By using this tool, uploads are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account security settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the tool's folder removes all locally stored tokens and logs.
